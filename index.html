What the shop's API does

  • POST/api/signup

    Open an account, with an address and any allergies.

  • POST/api/orders

    Place an order, pay for it and get a receipt by email.

  • POST/api/support

    Ask for help with an order.

  • POST/api/close-account

    Close an account and remove its details.

  • GET/api/nightly-report

    The day's report, sent every morning at 06:00 UTC.
